About 4ps

MP4 60 FPS Lossless Optimization Utility

4ps patches MP4 video container atom headers so TikTok's server pipeline ingests at pristine 60 FPS visual quality without downsampling or frame dropping.

All video frames and audio packets are copied natively in your browser using WebAssembly. Your videos are never uploaded to any third-party server.
